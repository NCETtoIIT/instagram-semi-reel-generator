  • Poor interior design ke kaaran customers kam der rukte hain aur repeat visits kam ho jaate hain, jisse loyalty nahi banti.
  • Unappealing photos ya messy menu layouts se customer experience kharab hota hai aur sales par negative impact padta hai.
  • Marketing budget waste ho jaata hai jab branding effective nahi hoti aur expected ROI nahi milta.

  • Generic branding messages (jaise 'great coffee and friendly service') se competitors se differentiate karna mushkil hota hai.
