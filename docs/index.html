📊 Statistical Proof
  • 75% log kisi business ki credibility uski website se judge karte hain. Healthcare mein, jahan stakes high hain, yeh aur bhi important ho jata hai.
  • 61% log health related information ke liye internet use karte hain, jisse trust ek vital element ban jaata hai.
  • Patient decision-making mein trust sabse critical factor hai. Jo websites trust-building elements (credentials, testimonials) nahi dikhati, unki conversion rates 40-60% kam ho sakti hain.
  • 84% patients online review sites visit karte hain healthcare providers ko evaluate karne ke liye, aur 73% online reviews ko consider karte hain provider choose karte waqt.
  • 77% se zyada log apni patient journey search engines se shuru karte hain.
  • Agar website mein information aur transparency ki kami ho, toh 71% patients naye provider ki talash mein nikal jaate hain.
  • 53% patients expect karte hain ki wo bina kisi se baat kiye appointment book kar payein. Online scheduling ek top factor hai jo patients ko modern clinic feel karata hai.
  • Healthcare searches mein se aadhe se zyada mobile devices par hote hain. Agar website mobile-friendly na ho, toh patients lost ho jaate hain.
❌ Audience Pain Points
  • Patients ek poorly designed website ki wajah se search chhod dete hain ya care delay kar dete hain.
  • Outdated ya messy websites patients ko doubt mein daal dete hain, aur wo aapki practice ki quality ko website se judge karte hain.
  • Agar website par professional credentials, patient testimonials ya online reputation clearly visible na ho, toh conversion rates 40-60% tak gir jaati hain.
  • Patient acquisition mein frustration hoti hai jab marketing mein invest karne ke baad bhi website conversions nahi deti.
  • Confusing navigation, poor mobile experience ya inconsistent CTAs ki wajah se patients appointment book nahi karte aur competitors ke paas chale jaate hain.
  • Website par basic information (jaise accepted insurance, clinic hours) na milne par 71% patients naye provider ki taraf dekhte hain.

  • Kya aapki clinic ki website patients ka trust jeet pa rahi hai? Ya unhe confuse kar rahi hai?
