  • Google HTTPS ko ek ranking factor maanta hai, jiske bina aapki website search results mein neeche jaa sakti hai.

  • 60% small businesses cyberattack ke 6 mahine ke andar band ho jaate hain.

❌ Audience Pain Points
  • Customers ko 'Not Secure' warning dikhta hai, jisse unka trust toot jaata hai aur wo website chhod dete hain.
  • Google rankings gir jaati hain, jisse organic traffic aur leads kam ho jaate hain.
  • Sensitive customer data (login, payment details) hack hone ka risk rehta hai.
  • Brand reputation damage hoti hai aur sales kam ho jaati hain.
  • Cyberattacks se financial loss aur recovery costs ka bojh padta hai.
  • Industry regulations (jaise PCI DSS) comply na karne par penalties lag sakti hain.
  • Business owners ko lagta hai ki unka business chhota hai, isliye unhe target nahi kiya jaayega, jo ek galatfehmi hai.
