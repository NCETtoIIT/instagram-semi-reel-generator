🔍 Complete Research Insights
📊 Statistical Proof
  • Mobile devices 2025 tak 72% internet traffic drive karenge.
  • 2024 tak, global eCommerce market ka 73% mobile commerce se aayega.
  • Google ne March 2021 mein mobile-first indexing ko default bana diya tha, aur July 5, 2024 tak 100% websites ke liye yeh universal default ho gaya hai.
  • Agar mobile load time mein ek second ki bhi delay hoti hai, toh conversion rates 20% tak kam ho sakte hain.
  • Global web traffic ka 60% se zyada hissa mobile devices se aata hai.
  • 2024 mein, global web traffic ka 59.4% mobile devices se tha, aur Q2 2025 mein yeh 64.1% tak pahunch gaya.
  • Google ke according, 48% users maante hain ki agar website mobile-friendly nahi hai, toh iska matlab hai ki business ko customers ki parwah nahi hai.
  • Google ka kehna hai ki 1 se 3 second ka loading time badhne se bounce rate 32% tak increase ho sakta hai.
  • Cyber Week 2025 mein, 70% online orders mobile devices se place kiye gaye the.
❌ Audience Pain Points
  • Website mobile par theek se na khulne se customer competitors ke paas chale jaate hain.
  • Search engine rankings down ho jaate hain aur online visibility kam ho jaati hai.
  • Poor user experience se visitors frustrate ho kar website chhod dete hain (jaise ki slow loading, zoom karna, horizontal scroll karna, chhota text).
  • High bounce rates ki wajah se potential customers lose ho jaate hain.
  • Leads aur sales conversions kam ho jaati hain.
  • Business ki brand image outdated aur unprofessional lagti hai.
  • Local customers tak pahunchna mushkil ho jaata hai, khaaskar 'near me' searches ke liye.
  • Contact karna difficult ho jaata hai (e.g., click-to-call buttons ka kaam na karna).
