📊 Statistical Proof
  • Consistent brand presentation revenue ko 33% tak badha sakti hai.
  • Strong brand wale businesses mein growth ki sambhavna 20% zyada hoti hai aur wo 20% tak price premium charge kar sakte hain.
  • 75% consumers kisi bhi business ko uske design se judge karte hain.
  • Food ka visual appeal 74% respondents ke liye 'bahut important' hai, aur 78% food styling mein interested hain.
  • Achhi branding cafes ko crowded market mein alag dikhne mein help karti hai.
  • Strong brand identity Instagram aur Facebook jaise social media platforms par logon ko business se jodti hai aur return visits encourage karti hai.
  • Branded cafes mein customers zyada der rukte hain ('dwell time' badhta hai), jisse sales aur average bill size badhta hai.
  • Visual branding se recognition milti hai, positive impressions bante hain, emotional connection develop hota hai, aur professionalism dikhta hai.
❌ Audience Pain Points
  • Cafe owners ko market mein alag dikhne mein mushkil hoti hai, unka cafe 'bheed' mein kho jaata hai.
  • Outdated ya inconsistent branding jo cafe ke current vision aur personality ko reflect nahi karti.
  • Achhi coffee ya food hone ke bawajood bhi naye customers attract nahi ho rahe.
  • Branding inconsistent hai across different touchpoints (logo, menu, signage, social media).
  • Generic ya 'cliché' designs jo cafe ko doosre competitors jaisa dikhate hain, unique identity nahi ban paati.
  • Poor design ke karan customers business ko kam professional ya sasta samajhte hain.
